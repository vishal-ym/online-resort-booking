Frequently Asked Questions

What is the eco-construction story of Mud House Hampi?

All our cottages are constructed by local artisans using Hampi's traditional red clay mud, blended with natural binders like husks and jaggery, creating superior thermal insulation. The structural stone elements are responsibly sourced from non-heritage quarry zones, and roofs are thatched with coconut palm leaves.

How does the UPI QR payment system work for booking?

When checkout is completed, our system dynamically generates a payment QR code showing the exact payable total. You can scan this using GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or any banking app. Once completed, enter the UTR (Transaction ID). Our executive validates the UTR via the admin page and instantly triggers booking confirmation vouchers.

Are there modern amenities inside these rustic cottages?

Absolutely! Although we respect local heritage designs, we offer high-speed Wi-Fi, premium king bedding with organic linens, custom copper fans, organic bath amenities, and mini-fridges. The Premium Suite and Family Villa additionally feature private decks and open-air outdoor stone baths.
